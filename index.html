#About

I am interested in developing robust and interpretable computational methods for understanding gene regulation from complex genomic data.

During my military service as a medic, I became interested in how clinical decisions are made under uncertainty. That experience led me toward computational approaches and a second major in Computer Science.

At the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, I worked in Prof. Terry Furey's lab on epigenomic method development.

My current interests include statistical genomics, gene regulation, causal inference, and computational method development.
